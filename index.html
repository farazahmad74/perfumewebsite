Deg-bhapka distillation

How Kannauj itra is made

Kannauj still distils attar in copper pots over a slow fire, the same way it has for centuries.

1

DeghThe copper still

Fresh flowers or baked earth go into a copper pot with water, sealed with clay and heated over a wood fire.

2

ChongaThe bamboo pipe

Scented steam rises and travels through a bamboo pipe to a second vessel.

3

BhapkaThe receiver

The receiver holds sandalwood oil and sits in cool water. The steam condenses and its scent passes into the oil.

4

KuppiResting the attar

The attar rests in leather flasks so the last moisture escapes, then ages until the scent settles.
